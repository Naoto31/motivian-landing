WHY MOTIVIAN

It's easy to get caught up in everything.
And lose sight of what matters.

There's always more that needs to be done — more to answer, more to try, more tools promising to do it for you. The days fill up, the weeks blur, and the things we care about most quietly wait for "someday".

Always more to do

The list never ends. Finishing it was never the point.

Easy to drift

What's urgent gets the time. What matters waits.

Days that blur

Busy isn't the same as meaningful — and it's hard to tell from the inside.

Motivian is a space to slow down for a moment, and ask: what actually matters to me today?

HOW MOTIVIAN WORKS

Discover what matters.
Then make room for it.

Motivian isn't just about doing what matters. It's about discovering what matters to you, finding the words for it, and making room for it in your everyday life.

not a system.

NOTICE · ARTICULATE · MAKE SENSE

Put what matters
into words.

Most of us carry a vague sense of what matters. Motivian gives you a place to name it — the parts of your life you care about, the paths inside them, and why each one matters. Just writing it down changes how a day feels.

Places and Paths
Each Place — Family, Health, your craft — can hold Paths, and Paths can hold Paths. Go as deep as your life needs, one clear step at a time.
Connections, not categories
There's no right way to sort a life. A family trip can live under Family and also feed Travel — so you see how the things you care about connect.
Ideas — no pressure
Park small things you could do next to the Place or Path they belong to. Add one to today when there's room.
LIVE IT

One day. Three small moments.

A light rhythm for every day — three small moments to slow down.

MORNING
Start with intention

One line for how you want today to feel. Not a goal to hit — a direction to lean into.

DURING THE DAY
Take small steps

A few small steps toward what matters to you. Finished ones fold away so the day stays light.

EVENING
Look back

A short reflection: what you experienced, what you learned, what made the day yours. A few minutes, then let it go.

FLOW

Intentional — and still
on top of things.

Real life still comes with deadlines, errands and appointments. Motivian handles your to-dos properly — so they don't crowd out what matters.

Questions

Can I manage my to-dos in Motivian?

Yes. Capture tasks in To Plan, drag them into your week and tick them off as steps. The difference is that each step can point back to what it's for — so the to-do list never drowns out what matters.

Does Motivian use AI?

Not for your intention or your steps — deciding what matters is the part worth doing yourself. We're listening to feedback about where a light, optional helper might fit (reflection, for example), but Motivian won't decide for you.

What happens if I skip a day?

Nothing. There are no streaks to break. A blank day is just a blank day, and tomorrow starts fresh.

Do I need to know my “why” first?

No. Start with a name — “Family”, “Health”, “Travel”. The why can come later, often while you're living it.

What if I put something in the wrong place?

There's no wrong place. If you move a Path later, it doesn't mean you got it wrong — it means your view of your life got a little clearer. Everything inside moves with it.

What does it cost?

One plan with everything included: €4.99 a month, or €39 a year. Every account starts with 14 days free — no credit card required. Joining the waitlist is free and doesn't commit you to anything.

Where is my data stored?

In the EU (Frankfurt). Your intentions, journal and photos are private to you.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Motivian works on the web, and there's a mobile app too — your Life Map, today and your Moments stay in sync between them.
