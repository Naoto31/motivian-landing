WHY MOTIVIAN

You can do almost anything today.
That's exactly the problem.

AI can draft it. An app can track it. A feed always has more. The list of things you could do keeps growing — and the days quietly fill up with everything except what you care about most.

More options

Every new tool adds more you could start. Very few help you choose.

More noise

What's loud gets the time. What matters waits for "someday".

Days that blur

Busy isn't the same as meaningful — and it's hard to tell from the inside.

Motivian is one quiet place that asks, every day: what matters to you — and did today get a piece of it?

TODAY, ON PURPOSE

One day. Three small moments.

No system to maintain. Just a light rhythm that keeps what matters in the room.

MORNING
Set an intention

One line for how you want today to go. Not a goal to hit — a direction to lean into.

DURING THE DAY
Take a few steps

A handful of small steps, each tied to something you care about. Finished ones fold away so the day stays light.

EVENING
Look back, briefly

A highlight, how the day felt, one small thing for tomorrow. Two minutes, then let it go.

A LOOK INSIDE

Today, at the center of everything.

Your intention and steps sit at the center. What matters to you is always a glance to the right. A good day from the past drifts in on the left.

Let it shape today.

WHY I'M BUILDING THIS

There's more I can do than ever — more tools, more ideas, more AI that can do things for me. And yet the days kept slipping by without me spending them on what I actually care about.

I didn't want another system to manage. I wanted one quiet place that asks me every morning what matters, and lets me give it a small piece of the day. The deciding is still mine — that's the whole point.

Motivian is still early, and I'm shaping it together with the people who use it. If this sounds like something you've been missing, I'd love for you to join.

Questions

Is Motivian a to-do app?

Not really. There are steps to tick off, but the point is the why behind them: one intention, a few small steps, and the things that matter to you, always in view.

Does Motivian use AI?

Not for your intention or your steps — deciding what matters is the part worth doing yourself. We're listening to feedback about where a light, optional helper might fit (reflection, for example), but Motivian won't decide for you.

What happens if I skip a day?

Nothing. There are no streaks to break. A blank day is just a blank day, and tomorrow starts fresh.

What does it cost?

Pricing isn't set yet. Joining the waitlist is free and doesn't commit you to anything.

Where is my data stored?

In the EU (Frankfurt). Your intentions, journal and photos are private to you.

Can I use it on my phone?

Motivian starts on the web. Dedicated mobile apps are on the way.
