Questions

What happens if I skip a day?

Nothing. There are no streaks to break. A blank day is just a blank day, and tomorrow starts fresh.

Do I need to know my “why” first?

No. Start with a name — “Family”, “Health”, “Travel”. The why can come later, often while you're living it.

What if I put something in the wrong place?

There's no wrong place. If you move a Path later, it doesn't mean you got it wrong — it means your view of your life got a little clearer. Everything inside moves with it.

Does Motivian replace my calendar?

No — and it isn't meant to. Keep using Google Calendar or Outlook for meetings and appointments. Your calendar knows when; Motivian is where you keep why things matter, and what your days were actually like. There's nothing to connect.

Can I manage my to-dos in Motivian?

Yes. Capture tasks in To Plan, drag them into your week and tick them off as steps. The difference is that each step can point back to what it's for — so the to-do list never drowns out what matters.

Does Motivian use AI?

Not for your intention or your steps — deciding what matters is the part worth doing yourself. We're listening to feedback about where a light, optional helper might fit (reflection, for example), but Motivian won't decide for you.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Motivian works in your phone's browser, and your Life Map, today and your Moments stay in sync with your laptop. A native app for iPhone and Android is on the way.

What does it cost?

One plan with everything included: €3.99 a month, or €39 a year, VAT included. Every account starts with 14 days free — no credit card required.

What happens when the trial ends?

Nothing is charged automatically — there's no card on file. If you'd like to keep going, you choose the monthly or yearly plan. If not, that's fine too.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can cancel from your account settings at any time, and your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

Can I export or delete my data?

Yes. You can ask for a copy of everything you've written, and you can delete your account and all of its data whenever you want.

Where is my data stored?

In the EU (Frankfurt). Your intentions, journal and photos are private to you.
